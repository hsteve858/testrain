Parent & caregiver guide

Two days each week for this unit happen at home. Here's what your student needs, and roughly how long each will take.

This page is formatted to print cleanly — use your browser's print dialog to send home a paper copy.

Day 2

Watch + simulate — about 20 min

A short video and an online simulation. No materials needed, just a device with internet access.

Day 4

Vocabulary game — about 15 min

Play a vocabulary game and complete a short online check. No materials needed.

Day 6

Watch + journal — about 20 min

Watch a short video and write a weather journal entry. Pen and paper or a doc both work — checking today's Colorado Springs forecast together is a nice add-on.

Day 8

Forecast log — about 15 min/day for 3 days

Check a weather app or website together and log the temperature and any precipitation. No materials needed.

No purchases required

  • Every remote day in this unit uses things you already have — a device, or pen and paper.
  • If your student wants to redo the water-cycle-in-a-bag lab at home for fun, all that's needed is a ziplock bag, water, and a sunny window.
