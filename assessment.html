Assessment

Checking for understanding

Short formative checks run throughout the unit, building up to a summative model that satisfies the 5-ESS2-1 evidence outcome directly.

Formative assessments

Spread across the unit, these are low-stakes and give an early read on misconceptions before the final model.

Exit tickets

After each in-person day, checking understanding of that session's focus.

Day 4 vocabulary check

A short Google Form covering evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection.

Teacher observation

During the hands-on labs on Days 3 and 7, watching for hands-on misconceptions in real time.

Summative assessment: final model

Days 9–10. Students build a model — poster, diagram, or simple physical model — showing how the hydrosphere and atmosphere interact, then explain it to a small group.

Criterion Developing (1) Approaching (2) Meets (3)
